> We’re crushed when what we’re promised is revoked–as if that’s not allowed to happen.

I read _The Obstacle Is the Way_ during my first job as a web developer, a first that was brutal. Promises _were_ broken, and I _was_ crushed from the start. Inspired by the book, I tried to see every obstacle as a learning opportunity. Although it wasn’t easy to do, and I collected some bruises, I did learn a lot by sticking around.
