## Ingredients

| Amount  | Ingredient                        |
| ------- | --------------------------------- |
| 150 g   | fine almond flour                 |
| 50 g    | unsalted butter, softened         |
| 40 g    | honey                             |
| 1       | egg yolk                          |
| 10–12 g | speculaas spice (see blend below) |
| 2 g     | baking powder                     |
| 1.5 g   | fine sea salt                     |
| 1 tsp   | vanilla extract                   |

### Speculaas spice

| Amount | Ingredient                   |
| ------ | ---------------------------- |
| 5 g    | ground cinnamon              |
| 2 g    | ground cloves                |
| 2 g    | ground nutmeg                |
| 1 g    | ground ginger                |
| 1 g    | ground cardamom              |
| 0.5 g  | ground black or white pepper |

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 160 °C fan or 170 °C conventional. Line a baking tray with baking paper.
2. Mix the almond flour, speculaas spice, baking powder and salt in a bowl.
3. In a separate bowl, mix the softened butter and honey until smooth. Mix in the egg yolk and vanilla.
4. Add the dry ingredients and mix into a soft dough.
5. If the dough is sticky, chill it for 15 to 20 minutes.
6. Roll the dough into small balls of approximately 5–6 g each and arrange them on the prepared tray.
7. Flatten each ball very slightly with your finger.
8. Bake for 14 to 17 minutes, until deep golden brown. Watch closely towards the end, as honey browns quickly.
9. Leave the kruidnoten on the tray until completely cold. They will firm up and become considerably crisper as they cool.

## Notes

- For maximum crunch, turn the oven down to 100 °C after baking and dry the kruidnoten for another 10 to 15 minutes, then cool completely.
- The recipe already uses honey as its sweetener, so no sugar substitution is needed.

Makes approximately 45–50 kruidnoten. The spice blend makes approximately 12 g.

Prep: 20 minutes, plus 15 to 20 minutes optional chilling. Cook: 14 to 17 minutes.
