## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient                                 |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------ |
| 6      | chicken thighs                             |
| 1      | large onion, finely chopped                |
| 2      | garlic cloves, finely chopped              |
| 1      | green pepper, finely chopped               |
| 1      | carrot, finely chopped                     |
| 2      | ripe tomatoes (or 200 ml crushed tomatoes) |
| 1      | bay leaf                                   |
| 150 ml | white wine                                 |
| 150 ml | chicken stock (or water)                   |
|        | extra virgin olive oil                     |
|        | salt and black pepper, to taste            |
|        | thyme or rosemary, a few sprigs (optional) |

## Method

1. **Brown the chicken:** Season thighs with salt and pepper. Heat olive oil in a large pot and sear the thighs on both sides until golden. Remove and set aside.

2. **Sofrito:** In the same pot, add more oil if needed. Sauté onion, garlic, pepper, and carrot over medium heat until soft. Add grated or crushed tomato and cook 10 to 15 minutes until the water evaporates.

3. **Deglaze with wine:** Add white wine and increase heat for 2 to 3 minutes to cook off the alcohol.

4. **Stew:** Return the chicken to the pot. Add stock, bay leaf, and herbs if using. Cover and cook over medium-low heat for **35 to 40 minutes**, turning the chicken halfway. Add a splash more stock or water if needed.

5. **Finish:** Taste and adjust salt and pepper. For a thicker sauce, mash some of the vegetables with a fork or use a hand blender (optional).

## Cooking Time Options

| Method                 | Time             | Notes                            |
| ---------------------- | ---------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Stovetop (medium-low)  | 35 to 60 minutes | Up to 1 hour is fine             |
| Oven (covered, 160 °C) | 1.5 to 2 hours   | Keeps moisture in, great results |
| Slow cooker (low)      | 6 to 8 hours     | Perfect fall-apart texture       |

## Herbs

- **Bay leaf** — almost essential, adds depth and aroma
- **Thyme** — pairs beautifully with chicken, rustic touch
- **Rosemary** — use sparingly, very fragrant
- **Parsley** — add fresh at the end for brightness
- **Oregano** — especially good when using tomato in the sauce
- **Smoked paprika** — not a herb, but very traditional for smoky flavour

> **Tip:** Add dried herbs early (with the tomato or wine) so they infuse. Add fresh herbs at the start for flavour, and save a little to sprinkle at the end.

## Notes

- Serve with fried potatoes, white rice, or crusty bread to soak up the sauce.
- The stew improves if made ahead and reheated.
- Can be frozen for up to 3 months.

Prep time: 15 minutes. Cook time: 40 minutes.
