> Humans aren’t wired to be constantly wired.

## Takeaways I implemented

1. Instead of using text messages to keep in touch with friends and family, I now try to leave voice messages more often.
2. I sometimes leave my phone at home when I go outside.
3. I spend more time in solitude and parks.

## An action that I’m considering

Conversation office hours. I want to schedule a couple of hours a week during which my friends and family can call me, knowing that I’ll be available.
