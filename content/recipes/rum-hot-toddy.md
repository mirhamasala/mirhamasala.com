---
title: Rum Hot Toddy
description: A winter warmer with rum, honey, and lemon. Add more rum if you still feel cold.
date: 2023-11-27
serves: "1"
time: 5 minutes
---

Last week, while triumphantly unwinding in front of our already-lit Christmas tree, I was inspired to make a Hot Toddy. Since G doesn’t like the whiskey version much, I tried rum. And I think it’s even better.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 175 ml | hot water _If you have a temperature-regulated kettle, set it to 90 °C. Without one, you’ll have to wait longer until you can start drinking — that’s all._ |
| 45 ml  | rum _I like Zacapa No. 23. The better the quality of the rum, the better the drink._                                                                        |
| 10 ml  | honey (opt for raw, if available)                                                                                                                           |
| 20 ml  | fresh lemon juice                                                                                                                                           |
| 1      | lemon wheel                                                                                                                                                 |

## Method

1. Combine all the ingredients in a mug or heatproof glass. Stir until the honey dissolves.
2. Garnish with the lemon wheel.
