## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient        |
| ------ | ----------------- |
| 480 ml | warm water        |
| 15 ml  | dish soap         |
| 24 g   | baking soda       |
| 60 ml  | hydrogen peroxide |

## Method

1. Add the warm water and dish soap to a spray bottle and swirl gently to mix. Avoid shaking, as it foams up.
2. Add the baking soda and stir until dissolved.
3. Add the hydrogen peroxide and mix gently. The mixture will fizz a little.
4. Spray onto the affected areas of tiles, grout or sealant.
5. Leave to sit for 15 minutes.
6. Wipe or scrub the surface, then rinse with clean water.

## Notes

- Hydrogen peroxide degrades in light, so store the bottle in a cupboard and use it within a few weeks. Mix a fresh batch rather than keeping it for months.
- Do not mix with bleach or vinegar, and do not transfer to a metal container, as peroxide reacts with metal.
- Works well on grout and silicone sealant. For older black mould, a second application may be needed.
- Ventilate the bathroom while spraying and avoid contact with eyes.

Makes approximately 600 ml.
