### ☕️ Coffee

#### The Capsule Cafe

I like coming here for the specialty coffee and natural wines. Try a glass of the Uivo PT Nat Curtido from Folias de Baco. It's a pale yellow, cloudy wine with a slight fizz. Tastes like a sour beer or kombucha but wine.

[Site](https://instagram.com/thecapsule.cafe) · [Map](https://goo.gl/maps/gEQGcWyVqNnA5G5c9)

### 🌮 Eats

#### Balagan

Sporting a Mediterranean menu, blending Portuguese local produce with Middle Eastern flavors. Weather permitting, reserve a table on the ocean-front rooftop terrace. However, stealing a seat on the boulevard for a coffee, a bowl of chocolate tahini granola, and surf-watching isn't bad either.

[Site](http://www.balaganfood.com/) · [Map](https://goo.gl/maps/h96aX3qqqER7mRxy8)

#### Clube Naval

Fresh grilled fish served with whole potatoes baked in coals. Hidden at the beginning of the harbor, it's a local jaunt you'd surely overlook if it weren't for the line of locals waiting in front. Be sure to arrive early, as they don't take reservations

[Site](https://www.facebook.com/snackbarcnericeira) · [Map](https://goo.gl/maps/vkPq5W8cLLWS5DCs8)

#### Costa Fria

It could easily pass for a diner in Tel Aviv. Exorbitant wine prices (for Portugal), but the ever-changing menu of sharing platters makes up for it. And so does the in-house baked bread.

[Site](https://www.instagram.com/costa.fria/) · [Map](https://goo.gl/maps/WNvdnH4UAXLo5Erp9)

#### La Popular Taberna

I'm not exaggerating when I say Popular is one of the main reasons we fell in love with Ericeira. No matter the day, it's like stepping into a Woody Allen scene. There's a hidden cocktail menu (Try the Sharon), plenty of wine, and a delicious Spanish tapas menu.

[Map](https://goo.gl/maps/zf4k22PYVMHRCqHE6)

#### Onegai

Sushi featuring just the right layer of rice, wrapped around fresh and high-quality ingredients. Also available for home delivery through Ericeira Eats (takes a full hour to arrive, so manage hunger levels wisely).

[Map](https://goo.gl/maps/5n5d3VF3Hzh8Tq5A9)

#### Ribas

All the seafood you can fathom.

[Site](https://ribasmarisqueira.pt/menu-completo/) · [Map](https://goo.gl/maps/dkVdwT8kQWTxLWJE6)

#### Immerso

One of the hotel's recently-opened restaurants, Emme on Fire, features a menu prepared using coal, grill, or the wood-fired oven. Before that, and still, I like to hide in the lobby with a dry gin martini.

[Site](https://immerso.pt/) · [Map](https://goo.gl/maps/nheFQ38BUDQn6AYX7)

### 🍸 Drinks

#### Mar das Latas

Likely the best spot in town for a sunset drink (and typical Portuguese snack: canned fish). It gets even better in the summer months when the street is closed off to cars

[Site](https://qrcode.emenuk.com/menu/4f0ceafe09121ec6998da8d1682f4eed/pt) · [Map](https://goo.gl/maps/sHCs3M1V3WNzoKcp9)

#### Aethos

For a laid-back drink away from it all in a styled space with pastel beach-toned vibes.

[Site](https://www.aethos.com/ericeira/) · [Map](https://goo.gl/maps/3j1pnY637np4CLxb8)

### 🍯 Gourmet Groceries

#### Lento

For sourdough bread, and other slow food products. The owners  also organize a 'slow breakfast table' experience every once in a while.

[Site](http://www.shopmonja.com/) · [Map](https://goo.gl/maps/arGuQdsEB6xPtMKN6)

#### Terço do Meio

Shelves filled with sourdough breads, and a display counter stocked with pastries.

[Site](https://www.instagram.com/tercodomeio/) · [Map](https://goo.gl/maps/SFPZXxpQ3EFowcfBA)

### 🏛 Sights

#### Praia de Ribeira d'Ilhas {#praia-de-ribeira-d-ilhas}

One of the seven world surf reserves, with surf for all levels, and a bar and restaurant on site. It's also the starting point of the dunes trail to Coxos beach, which is stunningly raw.

[Map](https://goo.gl/maps/KM7WEJb1ucDowFci8)

#### Praia da Foz do Lizandro

My favorite beach in the area, long enough for a good beach walk. And with a river that runs into the sea at times.

[Map](https://goo.gl/maps/rQW735mYntM7sb3x7)

#### Praia dos Coxos

A stunning beach to which you can walk from or to Ribeira d'Ilhas. It's an excellent spot for watching surfers ride some impressive waves.

[Map](https://goo.gl/maps/QTadVaQZDqGLWkWo9)

#### Praia de São Lourenço {#praias-de-sao-lourenco}

The sand and cliffs that made me fall in love with the Ericeira area.

[Map](https://goo.gl/maps/2TQjFx9GMEHwYy3C9)

#### Praia de São Julião {#praia-de-sao-juliao}

Another bit of sand worth a visit.

[Map](https://goo.gl/maps/xUXChARQ3h2VzEVw7)

### 🍦 Sweets

#### O Pãozinho das Marias {#o-paozinho-das-marias}

We've tried the pastéis de nata (Portuguese custard tarts) from Lisbon to Belem, but the one from Maria, with its balanced sweetness, caramelized top, and shatteringly crisp pastry, without a doubt, is our favorite.

[Site](https://www.facebook.com/opaozinhodasmarias/) · [Map](https://goo.gl/maps/VxLYW4qy5hR2t42r6)
