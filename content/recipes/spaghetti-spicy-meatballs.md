## Ingredients

### Meatballs

| Amount | Ingredient                                |
| ------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| 80 ml  | milk                                      |
| 1      | slice bread, crust removed                |
| 500 g  | beef mince (or mixed beef-and-pork mince) |
| 1      | small onion, finely chopped               |
| 2 tbsp | fresh flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped   |
| 1 tsp  | fresh thyme, finely chopped               |
| 1      | egg, lightly beaten                       |
| 25 g   | Parmesan, freshly grated                  |
| 2      | garlic cloves, finely chopped             |
| 2      | fresh red chillies, finely chopped        |
|        | sea salt and freshly ground black pepper  |

### Sauce and pasta

| Amount | Ingredient                                                        |
| ------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 60 ml  | olive oil                                                         |
| 800 g  | canned Roma tomatoes (2 × 400 g cans), chopped, with their juices |
| 50 g   | fresh basil leaves, torn                                          |
| 500 g  | spaghetti                                                         |
|        | fresh basil leaves, extra, to serve                               |
|        | Parmesan, freshly grated, to serve                                |

## Method

1. Warm the milk and bread in a small saucepan over low heat. Once the bread has absorbed the milk, remove it from the heat, mash it to a pulp with a fork and leave to cool.
2. Put the mince, onion, parsley, thyme, egg, Parmesan, garlic, half the chopped chilli and the bread mixture in a large bowl. Season with salt and black pepper, then mix with damp hands until well combined.
3. Shape the mixture into about 24 meatballs, each roughly 4 cm in diameter.
4. Heat the olive oil in a large frying pan over medium heat. Brown the meatballs all over, then lift them out and pour away any excess oil.
5. Add the tomatoes and their juices, the remaining chilli and half the torn basil to the pan. Season, bring to the boil, then return the meatballs to the sauce. Simmer for about 20 minutes, until cooked through.
6. Meanwhile, cook the spaghetti in a large pan of salted boiling water until al dente. Drain well.
7. Divide the spaghetti among four warm plates and spoon over the meatballs and sauce. Finish with the remaining torn basil, extra basil leaves and grated Parmesan.

## Notes

- For a milder version, use one chilli or remove the seeds.
