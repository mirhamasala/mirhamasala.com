## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient                 |
| ------ | -------------------------- |
| 120 g  | all-purpose flour          |
| 2      | eggs                       |
| 350 ml | milk                       |
| 30 g   | butter, melted             |
| 1 tbsp | pure maple syrup           |
|        | salt, a pinch              |
| 1 tsp  | vanilla extract            |
|        | butter, extra, for the pan |

## Method

1. Whisk all ingredients together until smooth. Rest the batter for 30 minutes.
2. Heat a lightly buttered pan over medium-high heat.
3. Pour approximately 60 ml of batter into the pan and swirl to coat it thinly.
4. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes per side, until lightly golden.

## Notes

- Resting the batter is key — it relaxes the gluten and gives smoother crepes.
- Serve with jam, chocolate spread, fresh fruit, or whipped cream.
- Stack with parchment paper between crepes to prevent sticking.

Makes 6–8 crepes.
