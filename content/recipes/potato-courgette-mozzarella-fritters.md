## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient                               |
| ------ | ---------------------------------------- |
| 300 g  | potatoes, coarsely grated                |
| 300 g  | courgettes, coarsely grated              |
| 4      | spring onions, chopped                   |
| 125 g  | mozzarella, grated                       |
| 15 g   | flat-leaf parsley, roughly chopped       |
| 2      | eggs, lightly beaten                     |
| 25 g   | plain flour                              |
|        | sea salt and freshly ground black pepper |
| 60 ml  | light-flavoured oil, for frying          |
|        | thick Greek yoghurt, to serve            |
|        | paprika, for sprinkling                  |
|        | lemon wedges, to serve                   |

## Method

1. Squeeze the grated potato and courgette very well to remove as much moisture as possible.
2. Put them in a bowl with the spring onions, mozzarella, parsley and eggs, then stir lightly to combine.
3. Stir in the flour and season with sea salt and black pepper.
4. Heat the oil in a large non-stick frying pan over medium-high heat.
5. Drop spoonfuls of the mixture into the hot oil and flatten them gently with the back of a spoon.
6. Cook for about 2 minutes on each side, or until golden brown. Work in batches so the pan is not crowded.
7. Drain on kitchen paper and serve with Greek yoghurt sprinkled with paprika and lemon wedges.

## Notes

- Serve on their own with drinks, as a vegetable side with grilled meat, or with bacon for breakfast.
- The drier the grated potato and courgette, the crisper the fritters will be.

Makes 12 fritters.

Prep: 15 minutes. Cook: 20 minutes.
