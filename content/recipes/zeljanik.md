## Ingredients

| Amount   | Ingredient                                               |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2–3 tbsp | soured cream (povlaka)                                   |
| 2–3 tbsp | feta cheese (or soft curd cheese), crumbled              |
| 1 tbsp   | plain yoghurt                                            |
| 10 g     | baking powder (1 sachet)                                 |
| 1 tbsp   | oil                                                      |
| 2        | eggs                                                     |
|          | milk, a little                                           |
|          | plain flour, enough to bring together into a soft batter |
|          | oil, extra, for the tray                                 |

### Finishing

| Amount | Ingredient                         |
| ------ | ---------------------------------- |
|        | butter, melted with a little water |

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to approximately 200 °C. Grease a baking tray with oil.
2. Combine the soured cream, cheese, yoghurt, baking powder, oil, eggs and milk. Mix well.
3. Add flour gradually, mixing by feel, until you have a soft, pourable batter.
4. Pour onto the greased tray and bake until golden and cooked through.
5. Melt the butter and stir in a small splash of water. Pour over the hot zeljanik as soon as it comes out of the oven.
6. Return to the oven for 30 seconds.
7. Remove, cover with a flat board or tray and leave to rest before serving.

## Notes

- This is a traditional Bosnian savoury pastry pie. Quantities are by feel (po osjecaju) - the original recipe is intentionally approximate.
- Povlaka is Balkan-style thick soured cream; crème fraîche is a good substitute.
