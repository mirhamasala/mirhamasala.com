## Ingredients

| Amount    | Ingredient                                      |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| 500 g     | ripe butternut squash or pumpkin, roughly diced |
| 400–800 g | canned black-eyed beans (1–2 cans), drained     |
| 450 g     | potatoes, peeled and roughly chopped            |
| 4         | ripe tomatoes, cut into wedges                  |
| 30 ml     | olive oil                                       |
| 1 tsp     | cumin seeds                                     |
| 2 tsp     | mustard seeds                                   |
| 1         | large onion, finely sliced                      |
| 2 tbsp    | Indian curry paste (preferably Balti)           |
| 2         | garlic cloves, crushed or sliced                |
| 1         | red chilli pepper                               |
| 600 ml    | vegetable stock                                 |
|           | fresh coriander, to serve                       |
| 1         | lemon, juice only, to serve                     |

## Method

1. Heat the olive oil in a large pan. Add the cumin and mustard seeds and cook for 1 minute until they begin to pop.
2. Add the onion, garlic and chilli and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until softened.
3. Stir in the potatoes and cook for 3 minutes.
4. Add the curry paste and vegetable stock, bring to the boil and simmer for 5 minutes.
5. Add the squash or pumpkin. Simmer for a further 15 minutes until the vegetables are tender.
6. Add the black-eyed beans and tomatoes and cook for 2 to 3 minutes more. Season to taste.
7. Serve on rice or couscous, garnished with coriander and a squeeze of lemon.

## Notes

The mustard seeds will pop and splutter - keep a lid nearby.

Prep: 15 minutes. Cook: 30 minutes.
