## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient                                               |
| ------ | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| 500 g  | potatoes, peeled and grated                              |
| 1      | small red onion, finely chopped                          |
| 10 g   | baking powder (1 sachet)                                 |
|        | water, a little, if needed                               |
|        | salt and pepper                                          |
|        | plain flour, enough to bring together into a soft batter |
|        | oil, for the tray and batter                             |

### Finishing

| Amount | Ingredient     |
| ------ | -------------- |
|        | butter, melted |

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to approximately 200 °C. Grease a baking tray with oil.
2. Combine the grated potato, onion, baking powder, salt, pepper and a drizzle of oil. Add flour gradually, mixing until you have a thick, spreadable batter. Add a little water if needed to bring it together.
3. Pour and spread onto the greased tray.
4. Bake until cooked through and golden on top.
5. As soon as it comes out of the oven, pour melted butter over the top and return to the oven for 30 seconds.
6. Remove from the oven, sprinkle with a little water, cover with a flat board or tray and leave to rest for 5 to 10 minutes before serving.

## Notes

- Quantities are by feel (po osjecaju) - the original recipe is intentionally approximate.
- Kljukusa is a traditional Bosnian home comfort food, similar to a potato bread or thick potato pancake baked in a tray.
