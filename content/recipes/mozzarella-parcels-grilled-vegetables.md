## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient                                    |
| ------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| 2      | balls mozzarella                              |
| 1      | lemon, scrubbed and zested                    |
| 100 g  | green asparagus tips                          |
| 1      | aubergine, sliced lengthways into thin strips |
| 1      | red pepper, cut into strips                   |
| 2      | onions, cut into thin wedges                  |
| 60 ml  | olive oil                                     |
| 2 tbsp | fresh thyme leaves                            |
| 8      | slices Parma ham (2 per mozzarella half)      |
|        | salt and pepper                               |

## Method

1. Halve the mozzarella. Juice the lemon. Marinate the mozzarella in half the lemon juice, the zest, salt and pepper.
2. Trim the asparagus tips. Toss with the aubergine, red pepper, onion and 30-45 ml of oil in a bowl.
3. Heat a grill pan and grill the vegetables in 2-3 batches until just tender.
4. Transfer the grilled vegetables to a bowl with the thyme. Toss with the remaining lemon juice and season. Cover with foil to keep warm.
5. Remove the mozzarella from the marinade and pat dry. Wrap each half in 2 slices of Parma ham so it is fully covered. Secure with a cocktail stick if needed.
6. Heat the remaining oil in a frying pan and fry the parcels over medium-high heat for 4 to 5 minutes, turning, until the ham is crispy and the mozzarella starts to melt.
7. Divide the vegetables between 4 plates and top each with a mozzarella parcel.
