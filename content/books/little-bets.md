> The little bets approach is about using negativity to positive effect. If your plans fall apart, refine them; if you don’t know where best to begin, just begin somewhere. Every decision is a risk: take a chance and see what happens.

**Inspired me to:** Keep meeting creatives in various design fields as I had started doing after reading [Designing Your Life](/books/designing-your-life).

**Parenting takeaway:** Parents of successful creatives “tend to support their kids’ natural pursuits, while also stressing work ethic and quality.”

**Book recommendation I followed:** [Mindset](https://www.amazon.com/Mindset-Psychology-Carol-S-Dweck/dp/0345472322) by Carol S. Dweck

**Recommended TED talks I watched:** [Do Schools Kill Creativity?](https://www.ted.com/talks/sir_ken_robinson_do_schools_kill_creativity) by Sir Ken Robinson and [Your Elusive Creative Genius](https://www.ted.com/talks/elizabeth_gilbert_your_elusive_creative_genius) by Elizabeth Gilbert

**Cheeky fact:** Inspired by the title of this book, I made [Slow Retreats](/posts/the-making-of-slow-retreats/) happen. Though I only actually read the book after the retreat.
