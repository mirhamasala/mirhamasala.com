## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient                            |
| ------ | ------------------------------------- |
| 25 ml  | oil                                   |
| 25 g   | butter                                |
| 2      | large onions, chopped                 |
| 1      | green pepper, seeded and diced        |
| 2      | garlic cloves, crushed                |
| 2      | celery sticks, thinly sliced          |
| 3      | carrots, coarsely grated              |
| 10 g   | curry powder                          |
| 5 g    | ground cumin                          |
| 5 g    | turmeric                              |
| 5 g    | ground fennel                         |
| 375 g  | brown lentils                         |
| 750 ml | water                                 |
| 1      | bay leaf                              |
| 100 g  | fresh parsley, chopped                |
| 5 g    | salt                                  |
| 10 g   | brown sugar                           |
| 175 g  | tomato puree                          |
|        | sour cream, to serve                  |
|        | chives and/or cashew nuts, to garnish |
| 1      | banana, to serve (optional)           |

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C.
2. Heat the oil and butter and saute the onions, green pepper, garlic, celery and carrots until softened.
3. Add the spices and stir over low heat for a few minutes.
4. Add the lentils, water, bay leaf, parsley, salt, sugar and tomato puree. Mix well and spoon into a large baking dish.
5. Cover and bake at 180 °C for about 1 hour, or until the lentils are soft, stirring once or twice.
6. Remove from the oven and swirl in a few spoons of sour cream. Sprinkle with chives and/or cashew nuts and return to the oven for another 5 minutes.

## Swaps & Substitutions

- Sugar: Replace the 10 g of brown sugar with 8 g of honey (honey is sweeter, so use ¾ the amount). Add it with the other liquids. The flavour will be slightly more floral but works well with the spice mix.

## Notes

Good served with sliced banana on the side.
