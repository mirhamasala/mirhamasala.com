## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient                                        |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------- |
| 2      | medium onions                                     |
| 1      | garlic clove                                      |
|        | olive oil, for frying                             |
| 2 tsp  | chilli powder                                     |
| 1      | fresh red chilli, deseeded and finely chopped     |
| 1 tsp  | ground cumin (or crushed cumin seeds)             |
|        | sea salt and freshly ground black pepper          |
| 455 g  | minced beef                                       |
| 200 g  | sun-dried tomatoes in oil                         |
| 800 g  | canned tomatoes (2 × 400 g cans)                  |
| ½      | cinnamon stick                                    |
| 800 g  | canned red kidney beans (2 × 400 g cans), drained |
| 200 ml | water (approximately)                             |

## Method

1. Blitz the onions and garlic in a food processor until finely chopped. Fry them in a little olive oil until soft.
2. Add the chilli powder, fresh chilli, cumin and a little seasoning. Add the minced beef and cook, stirring, until browned.
3. Blitz the sun-dried tomatoes with enough oil from the jar to make a loose paste.
4. Add the paste to the beef with the canned tomatoes, cinnamon stick and about 200 ml water. Season again if needed.
5. Bring to the boil, cover first with baking paper and then with a lid. Either simmer over low heat for 1½ hours or transfer the covered pan to the oven for the same amount of time.
6. Add the kidney beans 30 minutes before the end; they are already cooked and only need to heat through.

## Notes

- Serve with rice, tortillas or crusty bread.

Prep: 20 minutes. Cook: 1 hour 30 minutes.
