## Ingredients

| Amount  | Ingredient                                |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| 30 ml   | oil                                       |
|         | chicken breast fillets, cut into pieces   |
| 3 tbsp  | curry paste                               |
| 600 ml  | coconut milk                              |
| 37.5 ml | fish sauce                                |
| 2 tbsp  | brown sugar                               |
|         | potatoes, peeled and cubed                |
| ½ tsp   | salt                                      |
| ½ tsp   | lime juice                                |
|         | green peas and fine green beans, blanched |
|         | fresh basil and coriander, to serve       |
|         | rice, to serve                            |

## Method

1. Parboil the potatoes until nearly tender and blanch the peas and green beans. Drain and set aside.
2. Heat the oil in a pan and brown the chicken pieces. Transfer them to a bowl.
3. Fry the curry paste in the same pan for 30 seconds.
4. Add the coconut milk, fish sauce and brown sugar, then return the chicken to the pan. Simmer gently for 20 to 25 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through.
5. Add the potatoes, peas and green beans in time to heat them through without overcooking them.
6. Season with the salt and lime juice.
7. Serve with rice and garnish with fresh basil and coriander.

## Swaps & Substitutions

- Brown sugar → honey: replace the 2 tbsp brown sugar with 22 ml honey. Add it with the coconut milk. No other liquid adjustment is needed for this curry.

## Notes

- The original recipe does not specify quantities for the chicken, potatoes, peas, green beans, herbs or rice; use enough for four servings.
