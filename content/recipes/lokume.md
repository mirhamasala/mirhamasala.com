## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient                          |
| ------ | ----------------------------------- |
| 300 g  | plain flour (approximately)         |
| 120 ml | plain yoghurt                       |
| 60 ml  | soured cream (or kefir)             |
| 10 g   | baking powder (1 sachet)            |
|        | salt, a pinch                       |
|        | oil, for frying                     |
|        | icing sugar, for serving (optional) |

## Method

_Instructions were not included in the original recipe. Lokume are a traditional Bosnian fried dough, typically made as follows:_

1. Mix all the ingredients together until a soft, slightly sticky dough forms.
2. Heat oil in a deep pan to approximately 170 °C.
3. Drop spoonfuls of dough into the hot oil and fry until golden brown on all sides.
4. Drain on kitchen paper and serve warm, dusted with icing sugar if desired.

## Notes

- Kiseljak in the original likely refers to a soured milk product (like kefir or buttermilk).
