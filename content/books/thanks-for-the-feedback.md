> Receiving feedback well means engaging in the conversation skillfully and making thoughtful choices about whether and how to use the information and what you’re learning. It’s about managing your emotional triggers so that you can take in what the other person is telling you, and being open to seeing yourself in new ways.

**Helpful insight**: There are three types of feedback: “Appreciation (thanks), coaching (here’s a better way to do it), and evaluation (here’s where you stand).” If we know what form of feedback we need, we should ask for it. Getting one type of feedback while we need another isn’t helpful.

**Most important takeaway:** Having a growth mindset is essential for many things in life, from developing skills and overcoming crises to receiving feedback and doing well socially.
