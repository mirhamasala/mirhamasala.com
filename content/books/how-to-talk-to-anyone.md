**Few favorite tips:**

**1. Never the naked thank you** – “Never let the words _thank you_ stand naked and alone. Always make it thank you _for_ something.”

**2. The broken record** – “Whenever someone persists in questioning you on an unwelcome subject, simply repeat your original response. Use precisely the same words in precisely the same tone of voice.”

**3. Bluffing for bargains** – “Your price is much lower when you know how to deal. Before every big purchase, find several vendors – a few to learn from and one to buy from.”
