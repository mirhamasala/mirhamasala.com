I'm not exaggerating when I say Popular is one of the main reasons we fell in love with Ericeira. No matter the day, it's like stepping into a Woody Allen scene. There's a hidden cocktail menu (Try the Sharon), plenty of wine, and a delicious Spanish tapas menu.
