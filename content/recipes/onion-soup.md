## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient                          |
| ------ | ----------------------------------- |
| 8      | white onions, finely chopped        |
| 500 ml | dry white wine                      |
| 2 l    | water                               |
| 6      | baguette croutons                   |
|        | Gruyère (or similar cheese), grated |
|        | olive oil                           |
|        | salt and pepper                     |

## Method

1. Saute the onions in olive oil until golden brown.
2. Pour in the white wine and reduce to one third.
3. Add 2 l of water and simmer gently for 20 minutes, until the onions are very soft and the soup is a nice brown colour. Season with salt and pepper.
4. Just before serving, top the croutons with grated cheese and place under the grill until golden brown.

## Notes

Translated from Dutch. Serve the cheese croutons floating on top of the soup.
