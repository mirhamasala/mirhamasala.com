## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient                                       |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| 1      | pointed cabbage (spitskool), cut into 6 pieces   |
|        | veal cutlets (1 per 2 people)                    |
| 2–3    | carrots, quartered lengthways                    |
| 1      | onion, quartered                                 |
| ½      | tin chopped tomatoes                             |
|        | salt and pepper                                  |
|        | bay leaf                                         |
| 2      | potatoes, quartered                              |
|        | suho meso (dried or smoked cured meat), to taste |
|        | paprika powder                                   |
|        | olive oil                                        |
|        | butter                                           |
|        | water                                            |

### Roux (zaprska)

| Amount | Ingredient  |
| ------ | ----------- |
|        | olive oil   |
|        | butter      |
|        | plain flour |

## Method

1. Boil the cabbage in salted water for 5 to 10 minutes. Drain.
2. Heat olive oil in a large, heavy pot. Place 2 leaves of cabbage on the bottom.
3. Arrange the veal cutlets on top with small knobs of butter.
4. In a separate pan, braise the suho meso, paprika powder, onion, tomatoes, salt and pepper together.
5. Layer the cabbage pieces over the meat, add the bay leaf.
6. Tuck the carrots in between the layers, then add another layer of cabbage and the potatoes.
7. Pour the tomato mixture over everything.
8. Place a smaller lid directly on the ingredients and add water to partially cover.
9. Cook on very low heat for 1½ hours.
10. To make the roux (zaprska): heat olive oil and butter in a small pan, whisk in flour and cook until lightly golden. Stir into the pot during the final minutes of cooking to thicken the sauce.

## Notes

- Suho meso is a Balkan dried/smoked cured meat. Smoked sausage or pancetta can be used as a substitute.
- The zaprska (roux) at the end gives the dish a slightly thickened, rich sauce.
