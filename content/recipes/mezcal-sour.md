## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient                    |
| ------ | ----------------------------- |
| 80 ml  | mezcal                        |
| 30 ml  | fresh lemon juice             |
| 20 ml  | fresh lime juice              |
| 30 ml  | honey                         |
| 1      | egg white                     |
|        | salt, a tiny pinch            |
|        | ice, for shaking              |
| 5 ml   | honey syrup, extra, if needed |

## Method

1. Add the mezcal, lemon juice, lime juice, honey, egg white and salt to a cocktail shaker without ice.
2. Dry shake vigorously for 20 to 30 seconds.
3. Add ice and shake hard for another 15 seconds.
4. Taste before pouring. If the mezcal is particularly smoky or strong, add another 5 ml honey syrup and shake briefly to combine.
5. Double-strain into two chilled glasses and serve without ice.

## Notes

- Double-straining removes small ice shards and helps give the drink a smooth texture.
- This recipe contains raw egg white.
