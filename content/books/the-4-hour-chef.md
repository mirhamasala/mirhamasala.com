## A purchase I made after reading this book

[Unicorn Magnum Pepper Mill 6″](http://a.co/3ztLqod) (p. 112). You’ll want to add ground pepper to everything.

## A restaurant tip I followed and loved

[Limón Rotisserie](http://www.limonrotisserie.com/) at the Van Ness Avenue in San Francisco (p. 564). Have the rotisserie chicken.

## Cheeky fact

During the [10-day silent meditation course](/tsop/posts/meditator-month) that I followed, I wasn’t allowed to write. Using [The Method of Loci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Method_of_loci) (p. 99), however, I memorized my introspective adventures, which I was then able to recall when I wanted to write about them.

## Reading tip

_The 4-Hour Chef_ is quite a monster, and trying to read it from start to finish could overwhelm you. I recommend listening to the audiobook instead and referring to the written work when needed. Reading along for some parts is also nice. You can download _The 4-Hour Chef_ audiobook for free after you subscribe to [Tim’s newsletter](http://go.fourhourworkweek.com/10x-output/).
