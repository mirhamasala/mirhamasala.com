Sporting a Mediterranean menu, blending Portuguese local produce with Middle Eastern flavors. Weather permitting, reserve a table on the ocean-front rooftop terrace. However, stealing a seat on the boulevard for a coffee, a bowl of chocolate tahini granola, and surf-watching isn't bad either.
