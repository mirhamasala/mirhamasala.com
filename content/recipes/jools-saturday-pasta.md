## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient                                                                 |
| ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 500 g  | good-quality rigatoni                                                      |
|        | olive oil                                                                  |
| 1      | red onion, peeled and finely chopped                                       |
| 1–2    | fresh red chillies, deseeded and finely chopped                            |
| 1 tsp  | ground cinnamon                                                            |
| 1      | bunch fresh basil, leaves picked and stalks finely chopped (kept separate) |
| 800 g  | good-quality canned plum tomatoes (2 × 400 g cans)                         |
| 560 g  | good-quality canned tuna in olive oil (2 × 280 g cans), drained and flaked |
| 1–2    | lemons, zest and juice                                                     |
|        | Parmesan, freshly grated, a small handful                                  |
|        | sea salt and black pepper                                                  |

## Method

1. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium-low heat. Add the onion, chilli, cinnamon, and **basil stalks**. Cook for about 5 minutes until softened and sweet.
2. Turn up the heat, add the tomatoes and tuna. Break up the tomatoes with a spoon. Season with salt, bring to a boil, then simmer for about 20 minutes. Taste for seasoning.
3. Cook the rigatoni in salted boiling water until al dente. Drain, reserving some of the pasta cooking water.
4. Toss the pasta into the sauce with the torn **basil leaves**, a glug of olive oil, lemon zest and juice, and Parmesan. Mix well. Loosen with reserved pasta water if needed.
5. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- Use good-quality tuna — it really makes a difference.
- If you can find fennel tops, stir them in with the tomatoes.
- The cinnamon + chilli + lemon combo sounds odd but works brilliantly.
- No sugar in this recipe.
