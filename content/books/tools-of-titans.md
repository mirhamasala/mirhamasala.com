**The one sentence that changed everything:**

> If you don’t have cancer and you do a therapeutic fast 1 to 3 times a year, you could purge any precancerous cells that may be living in your body. –[Dom D’Agostino on Fasting, Ketosis, and the End of Cancer](https://tim.blog/2015/11/03/dominic-dagostino/)

**Podcast recommendation:** Ferriss largely adapted the profile chapters in [Tools of Titans](/books/tools-of-titans) from interviews that he did on his podcast, **The Tim Ferriss Show**. You can listen to these interviews for free online. Two of my favorite episodes are [Scott Adams: The Man Behind Dilbert](https://tim.blog/2015/09/22/scott-adams-the-man-behind-dilbert/) and [Seth Godin on How to Say “No,” Market Like a Professional, and Win at Life](https://tim.blog/2018/11/01/seth-godin-this-is-marketing/).
