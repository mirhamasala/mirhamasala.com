## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient                                                        |
| ------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1      | butternut squash (about 1 kg whole, or 750 g peeled and deseeded) |
| 45 ml  | extra virgin olive oil                                            |
| 2      | medium onions (about 250 g), cut into wedges                      |
| 2      | garlic cloves (about 6 g), finely chopped                         |
| 4 g    | ground cumin                                                      |
| 1 g    | red chilli flakes (adjust to taste)                               |
| 800 ml | chicken stock                                                     |
| 4–6 g  | salt (to taste)                                                   |
| 125 ml | crème fraîche, for serving (optional)                             |
|        | olive oil, extra, for serving                                     |

## Method

1. Peel the butternut squash, cut it in half lengthwise and remove the seeds. Cut the flesh into cubes of about 2 cm.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large, heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat. Add the onions and cook for 2 to 3 minutes until softened. Add the garlic, cumin and chilli flakes and cook for 1 minute, stirring constantly, until fragrant.
3. Add the squash cubes to the pot. Pour in the stock, bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer gently until the squash is very tender.
4. Blend with an immersion blender until completely smooth. Season with salt to taste.
5. Divide between four bowls. Add a spoonful of crème fraîche if using, and finish with a drizzle of olive oil.
