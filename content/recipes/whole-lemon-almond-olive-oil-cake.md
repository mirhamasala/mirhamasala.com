## Ingredients

| Amount   | Ingredient                                    |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| 1        | large lemon (preferably unwaxed or organic)   |
| 175 g    | fine blanched almond flour                    |
| 4        | large eggs                                    |
| 120 ml   | extra virgin olive oil                        |
| 80–90 ml | honey (use 90 ml if the lemon has thick skin) |
| 1½ tsp   | baking powder                                 |
| ½ tsp    | baking soda                                   |
| ¼ tsp    | fine salt                                     |
|          | fresh lemon zest, to finish (optional)        |

## Method

1. Put the whole lemon in a small saucepan, cover it with water and simmer for 25 minutes. Drain and leave it to cool until safe to handle.
2. Cut the lemon in half and remove any seeds, keeping all the flesh, peel and pith.
3. Heat the oven to 175 °C conventional or 165 °C fan. Grease and line a 20 cm round cake tin.
4. Put the entire cooked lemon in a blender with the eggs, honey and olive oil. Blend until very smooth and creamy.
5. In a separate bowl, combine the almond flour, baking powder, baking soda and salt.
6. Pour the lemon mixture into the dry ingredients and fold together only until fully combined.
7. Pour the batter into the prepared tin and bake immediately for 35 to 40 minutes, checking from 33 to 35 minutes. The top should be golden and the centre set; a skewer may have a few moist crumbs but no wet batter.
8. Leave the cake in the tin for about 15 minutes, then remove it and cool completely.
9. Just before serving, grate a little fresh lemon zest over the top for a brighter lemon aroma, if desired.

## Notes

- Do not rest the batter. Once the baking soda meets the lemon, get the cake into the oven promptly.
- The whole boiled lemon, including its peel and pith, gives the cake its pronounced lemon flavour.
- The original notes recommend skipping extra whipped honey; the cake is already flavourful and sweet enough.

### Original oat-flour version

- The original recipe from **@joey_wellness** used 1 large lemon, 120 ml extra-virgin olive oil, 120 ml honey, 3 eggs, approximately 140 g oat flour, 10 ml baking powder and a pinch of salt. It also suggested an optional 60 ml honey glaze.
- Its method was to boil the lemon for 25 minutes; blend it with the oil, honey and eggs; mix in the dry ingredients; cover and rest the batter at room temperature for 10 minutes; then bake at 175 °C for about 40 minutes.
- The version above adapts it for almond flour by using 175 g almond flour, 4 eggs, less honey, and a combination of baking powder and baking soda. Unlike the oat-flour original, the almond batter should be baked immediately rather than rested.

Prep: 45 minutes. Cook: 35 to 40 minutes.
