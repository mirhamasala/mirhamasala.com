## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient                |
| ------ | ------------------------- |
| 120 g  | cooked sweet potato       |
| 50 g   | frozen ripe banana        |
| 5 g    | cocoa powder              |
| 120 ml | milk                      |
| 1 tsp  | vanilla essence           |
| 1      | Medjool date (optional)   |
| 16 g   | almond butter (optional)  |
| 30 g   | protein powder (optional) |

## Method

1. Add all ingredients to a blender.
2. Blend until smooth.
3. Serve immediately, or store in the fridge for later.
