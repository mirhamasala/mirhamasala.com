## Ingredients

### Stew

| Amount | Ingredient                                              |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| 500 g  | Romano beans (or similar dried beans), soaked overnight |
| 1      | medium red onion, peeled and very finely diced          |
| 1      | carrot, washed and peeled                               |
| 15 ml  | oil                                                     |
| ½ tsp  | black pepper                                            |

### Roux (zaprska)

| Amount | Ingredient            |
| ------ | --------------------- |
| 2 tbsp | plain flour           |
| 45 ml  | oil                   |
| 1 tsp  | Vegeta seasoning      |
| ½ tsp  | salt                  |
| ½ tsp  | red paprika powder    |
| ½ tsp  | tomato paste          |
| 400 ml | water (approximately) |

## Method

1. Put the beans in a pot, cover with water and bring to the boil.
2. Once boiling, drain and rinse the beans.
3. Return the beans to the pot with the onion and carrot. Cover with water to your preference.
4. Add the pepper and oil, cover and cook until the beans are almost tender.
5. While the beans finish cooking, prepare the roux: in a small pan, cook the flour and oil together over medium heat for about 10 minutes, stirring, until the flour takes on a little colour.
6. Add the Vegeta, salt, paprika powder and tomato paste and cook together for another 5 minutes.
7. Add approximately 400 ml of water (more if you prefer a thinner stew) and continue to stir and cook until the water is absorbed and the roux is thick.
8. Stir the roux into the beans. Continue to cook for 20 to 30 minutes until the roux is fully incorporated.
9. Taste and adjust salt as needed before serving.

## Notes

- Romano beans are traditional, but any white or speckled bean works well.
- Vegeta is a common Balkan all-purpose seasoning blend. It can be substituted with a mix of salt, dried herbs and a little celery salt.
- The stew thickens as it cools.
