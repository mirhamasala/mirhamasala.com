My mom always made this when I had a cold as a kid. Nowadays, G and I have it for breakfast and sometimes after a light dinner. I like mine hot. G prefers his warm to preserve more of the nutrients in raw milk.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient       |
| ------ | ---------------- |
| 200 ml | raw milk         |
| 30 g   | grass-fed butter |
| 1 tsp  | raw honey        |

## Method

Heat all ingredients in a pot until the butter is melted and the milk has reached your desired temperature. Stir and serve.

_If you want a frothier cup, blitz the liquid in a blender for 30 seconds after heating. For a thicker beverage, add a raw egg while blending._
