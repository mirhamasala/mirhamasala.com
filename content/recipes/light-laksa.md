## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient                                            |
| ------ | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 tsp  | peanut oil                                            |
| 1 tbsp | red curry paste                                       |
| 1 l    | chicken stock                                         |
| 150 ml | coconut milk                                          |
| 2      | makrut lime leaves                                    |
|        | makrut lime leaves, extra, finely sliced, for garnish |
| 3 cm   | fresh ginger, peeled and finely chopped               |
| 500 g  | chicken breast fillets                                |
|        | light brown soft sugar, to taste                      |
|        | lime juice, to taste                                  |
| 150 g  | rice vermicelli, soaked in hot water and drained      |
| 1      | pak choi, sliced                                      |
| 90 g   | bean sprouts, cleaned                                 |

## Method

1. Heat the peanut oil in a large saucepan over medium heat. Add the curry paste and stir for 1 to 2 minutes, until fragrant.
2. Add the chicken stock, coconut milk, whole lime leaves and ginger.
3. Increase the heat and bring to the boil. Reduce the heat to very low, add the chicken, cover and gently poach for about 7 minutes, or until just cooked through.
4. Remove the chicken and leave it to cool slightly, then cut it into strips.
5. Season the soup to taste with brown sugar and lime juice.
6. Divide the rice vermicelli, chicken, pak choi and bean sprouts among four large bowls.
7. Pour over the hot soup and garnish with finely sliced lime leaves.

## Swaps & Substitutions

- Brown sugar → honey: because the sugar is added to taste, begin with a small drizzle of honey and add more as needed. As a guide, use about 3 ml honey for every 4 ml sugar; no other liquid adjustment is needed in this soup.

## Notes

- Keep the broth just below a simmer while poaching the chicken so it stays tender.

Prep: 15 minutes. Cook: 15 minutes.
