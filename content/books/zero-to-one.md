> Would-be entrepreneurs are told that nothing can be known in advance: we’re supposed to listen to what customers say they want, make nothing more than a “minimum viable product,” and iterate our way to success.\
> But leanness is a methodology, not a goal. Making small changes to things that already exist might lead you to a local maximum, but it won’t help you find the global maximum.

## Paradigm shift

Where most often, you’ll read that a startup should only want to be moderately original; iterate to success; improve on the competition, and focus on the product instead of sales, Peter Thiel believes we need to turn this advice upside down. He thinks successful startups should be bold; have a plan; stay away from competition, and put as much effort into sales as into product.
