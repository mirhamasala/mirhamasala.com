## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient                              |
| ------ | --------------------------------------- |
| 1      | pumpkin (or butternut squash)           |
| 1      | red chilli pepper                       |
| 45 ml  | sunflower oil                           |
| 2      | garlic cloves, finely chopped           |
| 2      | onions, cut into wedges                 |
| 2 tsp  | curry powder                            |
| 800 ml | chicken stock (from a cube)             |
| 125 ml | crème fraîche (you may not need it all) |

## Method

1. Halve the pumpkin lengthways. Remove the seeds and stringy interior, then cut off the skin with a sharp knife. Cut the flesh into 2 cm cubes.
2. Remove the stem from the red chilli. Halve the chilli lengthways and remove the seed membranes. Finely chop the flesh.
3. Heat the oil in a large heavy-bottomed pan. Saute the chilli, garlic, onion and curry powder for 2 minutes over medium heat. Add the pumpkin and cook, stirring, for 2 minutes.
4. Add the stock, bring to the boil and simmer on low heat for 15 minutes.
5. Remove from the heat and blend with a stick blender until smooth.
6. Ladle into 4 bowls and add 15 ml of crème fraîche to each bowl before serving.

## Notes

Good served with multigrain baguette. Translated from Dutch.
