## Ingredients

### Dough

| Amount | Ingredient                   |
| ------ | ---------------------------- |
| 500 g  | plain flour                  |
| 7 g    | dried yeast (1 sachet)       |
|        | olive oil, a generous splash |
| 300 ml | water                        |
| 1½ tsp | salt                         |

### Tomato sauce

| Amount | Ingredient                |
| ------ | ------------------------- |
| 45 ml  | olive oil                 |
| 3      | garlic cloves, minced     |
| 400 g  | canned tomato sauce       |
| ½ tsp  | crushed red pepper flakes |
| 2 tsp  | dried thyme               |
| 1 tsp  | Italian seasoning         |
| 2 tsp  | dried basil               |
| 2 tsp  | dried oregano             |
|        | salt, a pinch             |
| 1 tbsp | brown sugar               |

### Topping

| Amount | Ingredient                            |
| ------ | ------------------------------------- |
|        | potatoes, thinly sliced and pan-fried |
|        | goat’s cheese, crumbled               |
|        | fresh rosemary                        |

## Method

1. Make the dough: combine flour, yeast, salt, olive oil and water. Mix and knead until smooth. Leave to rest for 1 hour.
2. Make the tomato sauce: stir together the olive oil, garlic, tomato sauce, red pepper flakes, thyme, Italian seasoning, basil, oregano, salt and sugar in a saucepan over low heat. Simmer, stirring occasionally, for 45 minutes. For best results, refrigerate overnight before using.
3. Preheat the oven to 240 °C.
4. Roll out the dough and prick all over with a fork.
5. Spread the tomato sauce over the base.
6. Arrange the sliced pan-fried potatoes over the sauce. Scatter over the goat's cheese, season with salt and pepper and add rosemary leaves.
7. Bake for 15 to 20 minutes until the crust is golden and crisp.

## Swaps & Substitutions

**Sugar (in tomato sauce):** Replace the 1 tbsp of brown sugar with 11 ml of honey. Add it together with the other sauce ingredients. The honey blends in easily during the long simmer and balances the acidity of the tomatoes just as well.
