## Ingredients

| Amount   | Ingredient                             |
| -------- | -------------------------------------- |
| 1 l      | clean, filtered water                  |
| ¼–½ tsp  | cream of tartar (potassium bitartrate) |
| ¼–½ tsp  | pure sea salt                          |
| 15–30 ml | fresh lemon or lime juice              |
| 5–10 ml  | raw honey or maple syrup (optional)    |

## Method

1. Add the water, cream of tartar and sea salt to a large bottle or jar.
2. Add the fresh lemon or lime juice and the honey or maple syrup, if using.
3. Secure the lid and shake vigorously for 15 to 20 seconds, or stir well, until the cream of tartar and salt are completely dissolved.

## Notes

- Cream of tartar supplies potassium, while the sea salt supplies sodium.
- Chill before drinking if preferred. Shake again before serving if anything has settled.
- This is a general hydration drink, not a medically formulated oral rehydration solution. Seek medical advice for significant dehydration or dehydration caused by illness.

Makes 1 l.
