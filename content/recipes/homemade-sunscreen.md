## Ingredients

| Amount  | Ingredient                                           |
| ------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| 110 g   | grass-fed tallow                                     |
| 110 g   | coconut oil (or olive oil)                           |
| 30–60 g | beeswax pellets (less for a cream, more for a stick) |
| 16–32 g | non-nano zinc oxide powder                           |

## Method

1. Melt the tallow, coconut oil, and beeswax in a double boiler over low heat until fully liquid.
2. Remove from the heat and let cool for 5 to 10 minutes, until warm but not hot.
3. Wearing a mask to avoid inhaling the powder, stir in the zinc oxide.
4. Whisk thoroughly, ideally with a hand mixer, so the zinc oxide is evenly distributed and does not settle at the bottom.
5. Pour into jars and let set at room temperature or in the fridge.

## Notes

Makes 1 small jar.

- This mixture has no verified SPF. Its zinc oxide percentage does not establish an SPF rating. Do not rely on it for sun protection; use a tested, broad-spectrum, water-resistant sunscreen with SPF 30 or higher. See the [American Academy of Dermatology’s guidance on homemade sunscreen](https://www.aad.org/news/homemade-sunscreens-not-effective).
