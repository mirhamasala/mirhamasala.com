## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient                                  |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| 50 g   | lardons (streaky bacon pieces)              |
|        | oil                                         |
| 1      | onion, finely chopped                       |
| 1      | garlic clove                                |
| 500 g  | tomatoes                                    |
|        | dried oregano                               |
|        | salt and pepper                             |
| 500 g  | small courgettes, sliced into 0.5 cm rounds |
| 2 tbsp | breadcrumbs                                 |
| 1 tbsp | fresh parsley, chopped                      |
| 1 tbsp | fresh chives, chopped                       |
| 1 tbsp | fresh dill, chopped                         |
| 40 g   | Parmesan, grated                            |
| 90 ml  | cream                                       |

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 225 °C.
2. Fry the lardons in a little oil. Add the onion and garlic and cook until softened.
3. Add the tomatoes and oregano. Season with salt and pepper and cook down into a sauce.
4. Layer the courgette slices in a baking dish. Pour the tomato sauce over.
5. Mix together the breadcrumbs, parsley, chives, dill and Parmesan and scatter over the top.
6. Pour the cream over and bake for 20 to 25 minutes until golden and bubbling.
