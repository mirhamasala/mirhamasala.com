> When we don’t examine the deeper assumptions behind why we gather, we end up skipping too quickly to replicating old, staid formats of gathering. And we forgo the possibility of creating something memorable, even transformative.

When organizing [Slow Retreats](/posts/the-making-of-slow-retreats/), I wondered if I should take a more relaxed approach. Encouraged by Priya Parker, however, who advises against being a chill host, I enforced a [detailed plan](/posts/the-making-of-slow-retreats#daily-schedule) and [set of out-of-the-ordinary rules](/posts/the-making-of-slow-retreats#rules). Awkward before the happening but in the end, the guests loved both.
