## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient                 |
| ------ | -------------------------- |
| 120 g  | buckwheat flour            |
| 1      | egg                        |
| 355 ml | water                      |
| 3 g    | salt                       |
| 15 g   | butter                     |
|        | butter, extra, for the pan |

## Method

1. **Make the batter:** Whisk all ingredients together until smooth. Rest batter for 30 minutes.
2. **Heat the pan:** Heat a lightly buttered pan over medium-high heat.
3. **Cook:** Pour approximately 60 ml of batter into the pan and swirl to coat thinly.
4. **Flip:** Cook 1 to 2 minutes per side until lightly golden.

## Notes

- Buckwheat flour gives galettes their distinctive nutty, earthy flavour and is naturally gluten-free.
- Classic fillings: ham, egg and cheese (complète), mushrooms, spinach and goat’s cheese.
- Slightly thicker and crispier edges than sweet crepes.

Makes 6–8 galettes.
