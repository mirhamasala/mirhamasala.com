## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient                               |
| ------ | ---------------------------------------- |
| 100 g  | extra-dark chocolate, broken into pieces |
| 100 g  | butter, cubed                            |
| 2      | large eggs                               |
| 75 g   | caster sugar                             |
| 50 g   | plain flour                              |

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 175 °C.
2. Cut four rounds of baking paper to fit the bases of the ramekins and place one in each.
3. Melt the chocolate and butter together over very low heat, stirring constantly. Remove from the heat and let the mixture rest briefly.
4. In a separate bowl, whisk the eggs and sugar until thick and pale yellow.
5. Fold in the flour.
6. Pour in the chocolate mixture and stir well to combine.
7. Divide the batter among the ramekins.
8. Bake in the centre of the oven for 15 to 20 minutes. The centres should remain soft and slightly molten.
9. Rest for 5 minutes. Run a sharp knife around the edges and carefully turn out, or serve directly in the ramekins.

## Swaps & Substitutions

- Caster sugar → honey: use 55 g honey in place of 75 g sugar. Reduce the butter to 90 g and lower the oven temperature to 160 °C because honey adds moisture and browns more quickly. The cakes will have a slightly richer flavour.

## Notes

- These can also be baked in a muffin tin lined with paper cases for easier removal.
- Do not overbake; the soft centre is the point of the recipe.

You will need baking paper and 4 greased individual ovenproof ramekins. An apple corer is optional.

Prep: 15 minutes. Cook: 15 to 20 minutes.
